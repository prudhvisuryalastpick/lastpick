Questions

Good to know

Does the business get my phone number?

No. When you tap "I'm interested", the business sees only your first name. You can see the business's number and contact them yourself.

Why do offers end the same day?

LastPick is for today's deals: food before closing, rooms tonight, free slots this evening. Offers end by 11 PM and are removed at midnight. Hotels and travel run 24×7, for up to 24 hours.

Who can post offers?

Only businesses verified by the LastPick team, each in its own category, up to 3 posts a day. Any business can also post job openings for its own shop.

Does LastPick handle payments for the deal?

No. LastPick only connects you with the business. You pay the business directly. Never pay an advance to someone you haven't verified.

What does it cost?

For businesses, Business Pro is ₹499 a month. For customers, the Customer Pass is ₹99 a month.
